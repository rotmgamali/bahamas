# SerenitySpaces Villa Complex — Property Details

A private 4-villa complex in Freeport, Grand Bahama Island.

## Overview

- **4 interconnected main villas:** Agave, Coconut, Lime, Pina
- **Total main capacity:** 14 guests
- **Beds:** 9 across all four villas
- **Bathrooms:** 4 full bathrooms
- **Shared amenities:** Private pool, tropical gardens, BBQ grill, courtyard
- **Location:** Sea Breeze area, Freeport, Grand Bahama Island, Bahamas
- **Distance to beach:** 5-minute walk to Coral Beach (1/4 mile)
- **Distance to airport:** 25 minutes from Grand Bahama International Airport (FPO)

## The Four Villas

### Agave Villa — Poolside Luxury

- **Sleeps:** Up to 4 guests
- **Beds:** King bed + pull-out double sofa
- **Bathroom:** Ensuite with rainfall shower
- **Kitchen:** Basic (burners, microwave, coffee maker, small fridge)
- **Features:** Direct pool access, private patio
- **Best for:** Couples, executives, most-premium option

### Coconut Villa — Most Spacious

- **Sleeps:** Up to 4 guests
- **Beds:** Queen bed + pull-out double sofa
- **Bathroom:** Full bathroom
- **Kitchen:** Kitchenette (burners, microwave, coffee maker, small fridge, cookware)
- **Features:** Two-room layout, separate living area, lush garden views
- **Best for:** Small families, groups wanting extra space

### Lime Villa — The Heart of the Complex

- **Sleeps:** 3
- **Beds:** Queen bed in living/sleeping area
- **Bathroom:** Full bathroom
- **Kitchen:** Full chef's kitchen — the only villa with a full oven
- **Features:** 6-person dining table + kitchen counter for 4 more, full-size refrigerator, stove, oven, microwave, coffee maker, cookware
- **Best for:** Hosting the group, shared meals, cooking classes

### Pina Villa — Baby Grand Piano Studio

- **Sleeps:** 2
- **Beds:** Queen bed
- **Bathroom:** Full bathroom
- **Kitchen:** Kitchenette
- **Features:** Vaulted ceilings, exposed beams, rich wood tones, baby grand piano (not tuned but playable)
- **Best for:** Musicians, executives seeking the most atmospheric villa
