# SerenitySpaces Bahamas — Pricing

All pricing is in USD. The Bahamian dollar is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar, so there is no currency exchange involved for US-based guests.

## Nightly Rates

| Booking | Rate |
|---------|------|
| Entire 4-villa complex | $650/night |
| Individual villas | From $150/night |

## Fees

- **Cleaning fee:** $180 (one-time, per booking)
- **Minimum stay:** 3 nights

## Long-Stay Corporate Discounts

| Stay length | Discount off nightly rate |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Up to 7 nights | 10% |
| Up to 14 nights | 15% |
| Up to 30 nights | 20% |
| 30+ nights | 25% |
